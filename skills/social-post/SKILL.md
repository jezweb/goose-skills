# Social post

You can already write. This carries only what you can't infer: the channel shapes,
where the voice and facts live, and that nothing posts without a yes.

**Voice + facts:** the owner's voice and any product/claim facts come from the
cortex — `wiki/owner/voice.md` and `wiki/knowledge/` in an Office Town box;
otherwise ask. Never invent a stat, testimonial, or detail — pull it or leave it out.

**Channel shapes** (verify current limits per platform — they drift):

| Channel | What works |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Hook in line 1 (only ~1-2 lines show before "see more"); one idea; specific; light/no hashtags |
| X | One sharp, front-loaded idea; a thread only if it earns it |
| Facebook | Short and conversational; a human angle or a question |
| Instagram | Caption serves the image; hook first; hashtags at the end |

**The guard:** draft only — social posts are published, so never post. Leave the
draft for the owner; post only on an explicit yes. One idea per post; if you're
cramming, that's two posts.
